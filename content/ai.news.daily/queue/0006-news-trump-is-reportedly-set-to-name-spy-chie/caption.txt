Trump is reportedly set to name spy chief Jay Clayton as the White House's new AI czar, according to NBC News, CBS and CNN.

President Trump is expected to name Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton as the White House's AI czar, according to sources who spoke to NBC News, CBS News and CNN. The announcement could come on social media as soon as Friday.

Clayton would reportedly keep his job overseeing the nation's spy agencies and take on the AI role alongside it.

The White House has not confirmed the pick. An official told NBC and CBS that any personnel announcement will come directly from the President and called the reports "baseless speculation" until then.

The news follows Tuesday's White House summit, where leaders of OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Nvidia and SpaceXAI signed voluntary safety commitments that Trump called "morally binding." Clayton attended the meeting and later told CNBC that "superintelligence is a national security issue."

Clayton was confirmed as DNI this summer. He previously led the SEC during Trump's first term and served as US Attorney for the Southern District of New York. The last AI czar, David Sacks, stepped down earlier this year.
