ChatGPT can now virtually try on clothes for you: upload a selfie and see an outfit on yourself.

OpenAI is rolling out two new shopping features in ChatGPT worldwide: virtual try-on and Favorites.

A new Try On button in shopping results lets you upload a selfie or full-body photo and see how clothing or accessories would look on you. You can also upload a screenshot of an item and ask ChatGPT to try it on.

Favorites saves products you like to a Library in the app, next to your try-on images.

The features run on OpenAI's new ChatGPT Images 2.5 model, which the company says gives more natural lighting, richer textures and faster generation.

Google launched its own virtual try-on last year, so ChatGPT is stepping into a space Google and Pinterest already compete in.
