Napster co-founder Sean Parker is rebuilding Stability AI as an AI toolmaker for music professionals, backed by Sony, Warner and Universal.

Sean Parker, who co-founded Napster, is steering Stability AI away from its image-generator roots and toward music, according to TechCrunch, citing an interview Parker gave The Information.

The goal: make Stability the go-to source of AI tools for music professionals. Parker says he is playing by the rules this time, conceding that Napster's ask-forgiveness-later approach didn't work out.

In late August, Stability announced $76 million in funding from investors including Sony, Warner and Universal. The three labels also licensed their catalogs for AI training as part of the deal.

Since then the company has released three new audio models and AI music-editing software. The tools can generate full instrumental tracks or short snippets from text prompts.

Next up, Parker says, is an update that lets users hum a melody or beatbox a drum pattern to steer the music.

Parker joined an $80 million rescue of Stability two years ago, after overspending and internal turmoil led to founder Emad Mostaque's exit. His longtime friend Prem Akkaraju became CEO.
