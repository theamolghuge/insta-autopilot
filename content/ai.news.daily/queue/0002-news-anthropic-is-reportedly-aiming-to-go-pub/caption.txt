Anthropic is reportedly aiming to go public as soon as mid-November

Anthropic, the maker of Claude, wants to list on the stock market as soon as mid-November, Bloomberg reports, citing people familiar with the matter.

The company could begin marketing the IPO the week of November 9. According to Yahoo Finance, it is targeting a valuation of up to $2 trillion, which would make it the largest IPO of all time.

A follow-up Bloomberg report says Anthropic will host prospective investors on October 14 at its San Francisco headquarters, where a select group of institutions can question senior executives. Invitations have just gone out.

Earlier this week, Reuters reported that Anthropic's S-1 prospectus shows 2025 revenue grew 12x to roughly $4.6 billion.

If the timeline holds, Anthropic would go public ahead of rival OpenAI. None of the plans have been confirmed by the company.
